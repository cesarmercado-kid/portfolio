LET ME INTRODUCE MYSELF

I'm an Industrial Engineer with an MBA and ten years across data and business analyst roles in the utilities and insurance sectors. I work the full route: pulling and cleaning the records, running exploratory data analysis (EDA), building the model, and presenting the result as a clear recommendation.

My daily tools are SQL, Excel and Power BI, pivot tables and lookups for the quick checks, Power Query to shape the data, DAX for the measures behind each report, and Access for smaller relational sets. For deeper analysis I use Python and its main data analysis and visualization libraries, and I build the findings as documents and slides.

I hold a Data Science certificate from Correlation One, which strengthened my foundation in applied statistics, predictive modeling, and problem-solving with data.
