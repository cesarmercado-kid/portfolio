When users are lost Between days 15 and 20

97 of the 105 users were still active on day 14. Only 4 leave during the first week, against 29 in the six days that follow, and the base ends at 44 users (42%) on day 28. Retention effort placed after day 20 arrives once most of the loss has happened.

What sustains usage The weekly promotion cycle

Activity follows a seven-day period: page views average 3.59 on Fridays against 2.53 at the troughs. The cohort that tracks that cycle, G3, spends 48% of its active days on promotion pages, against 27% for the other two.

How to anticipate a dropout A fall in page views

In G2 and G3 the last active day sits well below each user's own average (-1.54 and -1.02 categories). In G1 the gap is 0.08, so for that cohort the trigger has to be the calendar rather than the behaviour.

Missing values

Once a usage variable turns missing, every later day is missing too and no user comes back. The missing values are not a defect to impute: they are the variable that measures dropout, and imputing them would erase the very thing being measured.
