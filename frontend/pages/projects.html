Descriptive statistics · Insurance

Health cost profile of smokers

What is the payment, personal and regional profile of a smoker? An analysis of 1,338 US health-insurance policies, read the way a competitor would read them to understand how it sets what it charges.

  • 1,338 policies, 7 variables, public Kaggle dataset
  • The smoking premium isolated from BMI, age and region
  • A negative correlation between age and charges, explained by Medicare rather than by the maths

Missing data · Product analytics

How a car app gets abandoned

How do users interact with a manufacturer's mobile app, and how does that change over time? 105 customers followed through their first four weeks, where the data that is missing is worth as much as the data that is there.

  • Monotone missingness treated as the measure of dropout, not imputed
  • Three usage cohorts recovered by clustering, plus a weekly cycle
  • Retention curve, missingness map and parallel coordinates

Time series · Energy policy

Energy consumption and generation

Given consumption patterns across sectors and over time, how should the U.S. Department of Energy allocate its resources toward nuclear generation? From the business question through to the recommendation, with the code that produces it.

  • 15,168 rows, 562 months of history, 1973 to 2019
  • 830 missing values dropped rather than imputed, all of them technologies not yet producing
  • Correlation by sector, then cut by decade and by season
