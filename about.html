Hi, I'm Cesar Mercado

I combine analytics, data, and business understanding to provide insights that guide decision-making across different functions.

I bring strong experience working with SQL, Python and BI tools to analyze data and create dashboards that drive impact. I have a track record of delivering actionable insights and building data products that solve real business problems across domains including insurance, finance and operations.

My work typically includes:

  • Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) and Statistical Analysis
  • Build BI dashboards and reports focused on KPIs
  • Build and publish interactive dashboards and Jupyter Notebooks documentation
  • Communicate insights clearly to both technical and non-technical audiences

Beyond the code, I care about clean documentation, and building solutions that are maintainable and understandable by everyone.
